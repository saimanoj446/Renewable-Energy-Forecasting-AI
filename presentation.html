Directive 2: Walk-Forward Rolling 'Time-Travel' Validation

Simulated Operational Time-Travel

  • Strict Historical Cut-Off: Model trained strictly on 2010–2013 data. Dec 14, 2014 is treated as unknown future.
  • Rolling Window Mechanics: At each hour h, model receives past lookback → forecasts next hour → window slides forward by 1h.
  • Multi-Start Ensemble: Each hour is predicted from multiple lookback starting points (t-24h to t-1h).
  • Uncertainty Bands: Averaging overlapping predictions yields a robust ensemble mean with ±1 std spread.
  • Test Accuracy: 1-step walk-forward R² = 0.9819 | MAE = 14.2 W/m² across the entire test day.

Directive 2: Multi-Start Prediction Error Convergence Proof

Sir's Hypothesis Validated

"As we move closer to the target hour, predictions will converge toward the actual ground truth."

  • Monotonic Error Decay: Forecast MAE drops steadily as the rolling window advances from 24h away down to 1h away.
  • Variance Compression: Prediction spread contracts by > 60% as lookback proximity increases.
  • Ensemble Averaging: Points predicted 10+ times exhibit significantly lower variance than single-shot predictions.

Directive 3: New Dataset Deep-Dive — Kuala Lumpur 1-Minute (2025)

Ground Station & Climate Regime

  • Scientific Source: Published in Data in Brief (peer-reviewed); recorded by outdoor solar research station, University of Malaya.
  • Coordinates: Lat 3.1390° N, Lon 101.6869° E (Elevation: ~60 m).
  • Climate Classification: Tropical Rainforest (Af) — near-equatorial, hot and humid year-round with frequent convective cloud transients.
  • The Acid-Test: Rajasthan has predictable direct sunlight. Kuala Lumpur experiences rapid solar drops (±400 W/m² in < 5 min). Proving our framework here proves it everywhere!
  • Instrument: Class-A Pyranometer recording GHI up to 1455.9 W/m² (capturing cloud-edge lensing).

Scale & Auxiliary Weather Pipeline

  • Temporal Resolution: Exact 1-minute intervals (720 records/day, 07:00:00 to 18:59:00). Full year 2025 (365 files = 262,020 rows).
  • Open-Meteo API Ingestion: Downloaded hourly weather: Temperature (21.2–37.8°C), Humidity (26–100%), Pressure (997–1009 hPa), Wind, DNI, DHI.
  • Time-Interpolation Pipeline: Hourly weather was linearly interpolated to 1-min intervals and merged with ground GHI (kl_hourly_weather.csv).
  • Feature Engineering: 29 features engineered (cyclical diurnal, lags 1 to 60, rolling averages 5 to 60).
  • Chronological Split: Train: Jan–Aug (73k seq), Val: Sep (9k seq), Test: Oct–Dec (55k seq).

Directive 4: The '8 Features × 60 Minutes = 480 Points' Rule

Mathematical Rule Formulation

  • Lookback Window (T=60): Captures 1 full hour of micro-ramp dynamics before forecast inception.
  • 8 Features per Minute (M=8): GHI, Temperature, Relative Humidity, DNI, DHI, Pressure, Hour_sin, Hour_cos.
  • Total Input Matrix: 60 time steps × 8 features = exactly 480 data points per window.
  • Optimal Complexity: Expanding beyond 60 min yields < 0.1% gain; shrinking below 60 min loses ramp context.
  • Deep Learning Tensor: Formatted directly as (batch_size, 60, 8) for sequence neural networks.

Directive 5: Continuous 1-Minute Walk-Forward Solar Forecast

Real-Time Roll Forward Performance

  • Minute-by-Minute Sliding: At every minute from 07:00 to 18:59, the LSTM ingests the past 60 min (480 points) to forecast +1 min ahead.
