3. IEEE CONFERENCE PAPER RECOMMENDATIONS:
   - The optimal lookback window across almost all horizons is x* = 30 to 60 minutes.
     * x < 15m lacks sufficient curvature acceleration.
     * x > 90m suffers memory dilution as tropical convective clouds decorrelate within 45m.
   - Present TWO distinct operating regimes in the paper:
     1. Primary Ultra-Short Ramp Regime (td = 5m - 15m): R² = 0.65 - 0.85 (High tracking for BESS frequency).
     2. Sub-Hourly to Hourly Dispatch Regime (td = 30m - 60m): R² = 0.55 - 0.62 (Unit commitment with Conformal CQR).
