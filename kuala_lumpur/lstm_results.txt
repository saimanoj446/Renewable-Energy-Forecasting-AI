1. DATASET CONFIGURATION:
   Location: Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia (Lat 3.1390° N, Lon 101.6869° E)
   Resolution: 1-Minute | Year: 2025
   Train Split: Jan 1 - Aug 31 (73,107 sequences)
   Val Split:   Sep 1 - Sep 30 (9,030 sequences)
   Test Split:  Oct 1 - Dec 31 (55,292 sequences)

2. PROFESSOR'S '480 DATA POINTS' FORMULATION:
   Lookback Window: 60 minutes (T=60)
   Selected 8 Features: ['GHI', 'Temperature', 'Relative Humidity', 'DNI', 'DHI', 'Pressure', 'Hour_sin', 'Hour_cos']
   Total Input Dimension: 8 features x 60 minutes = 480 points per sample
   Input Tensor Shape: (batch_size, 60, 8)
